V Self-Voicing Speech Toggles Web Speech API self-voicing narration.

Rules of Ludo

  • Starting Yard: All 4 tokens begin in your Yard. You must roll a 6 to exit a token from the Yard onto your colored start square on the main track.
  • Bonus Rolls: You earn an extra roll whenever you:
    1. Roll a 6 (Warning: rolling three 6s in a row forfeits your turn!).
    2. Capture an opponent's token.
    3. Successfully bring a token into the Home Goal.
  • Capturing: Landing on a square occupied by an opponent token captures it, sending it all the way back to their Yard, and gives you a bonus roll!
  • Safe Squares: Marked with a star (★) or located at each player's starting square. Multiple tokens of different colors can safely coexist on safe squares without capturing each other.
  • Home Stretch & Goal: After completing a full circuit of the 52-square track, your tokens turn into your 5-square colored Home Stretch. The central Home Goal (square 56) requires an exact roll. Overshooting is not allowed.
  • Winning: The first player to bring all 4 tokens into the Home Goal wins the game!
